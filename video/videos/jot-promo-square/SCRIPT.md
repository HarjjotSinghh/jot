# SCRIPT - jot-promo-square

**Voice:** Adam Stone (HeyGen)
**Voice direction:** A developer showing you something on their own machine, not
an ad read. Flat and factual; let the numbers do the lifting. No upward
inflection at line ends, no salesman warmth.

One spoken line per frame: audio.mjs writes one audio file per frame, so two
lines against the same frame silently overwrite each other. Sentences inside a
line are fine and read as a natural pause.

Each line is sized to its frame's window with headroom, so the shot can settle
before the cut rather than ending on the last syllable.

---

## Line 1 - The tax (Frame 1)

**Time:** 1.2 - 4.3s
**Delivery:** Recognition, not complaint. A shared experience.

    You have told your agents the same thing a hundred times.

## Line 2 - The logs (Frame 2)

**Time:** 5.3 - 11.3s
**Delivery:** The turn. Slight lift on "log file", then land the second sentence flat.

    Every one of those corrections is sitting in a log file. This reads all of them.

## Line 3 - The test (Frame 3)

**Time:** 12.3 - 17.3s
**Delivery:** Plain, then clipped. Three facts, no adornment.

    Then test it blind against yourself. Ten real decisions, answers sealed.

## Line 4 - The finding (Frame 4)

**Time:** 18.1 - 22.3s
**Delivery:** Even. Do not sell it; the drop from full marks to zero does that on its own.

    Rules mined from the logs scored full marks. Rules I reasoned into scored zero.
